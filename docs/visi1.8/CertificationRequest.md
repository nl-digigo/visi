## Procedure for applying for a VISI quality mark

Version 1.02, dd 22 March 2012

### Application for quality mark

-   The supplier who wishes to obtain the VISI quality mark must submit
    a written request to CROW via the application form on the VISI
    website. This allows all applicants to submit the application in the
    same transparant manner.

-   On the application form, the supplier declares by placing a 'check
    mark' next to the statement that he:

    -   is aware of the requirements and guidelines regarding the VISI
        system;

    -   agrees with the procedure for applying for the VISI quality
        mark;

    -   agrees with the contents of the VISI quality mark agreement. The
        supplier also states on the application form:

        -   its official company name with full (visiting) address;

        -   title, initials, surname and position of the representative
            who will sign the VISI quality mark agreement on behalf of
            the supplier;

        -   the email address of this contact person;

        -   the full name and surname of the software product for which
            the supplier wishes to obtain the VISI quality mark;

        -   the version number of this software product;

        -   the website where the product information can be found (if
            available).

Signing the quality mark agreement and issuing the certificate takes
place after the test has been successfully completed and the procedure
has been completed correctly.

-   The application form can only be submitted if it has been completed
    in full and if the supplier agrees with this procedure, with the
    requirements and guidelines, and with the content of the quality
    mark agreement.

-   This procedure, the MODEL of the 'VISI quality mark agreement for
    VISI-compatible software' as well as the requirements and guidelines
    that the software product must meet to obtain the VISI quality mark,
    can be freely downloaded from the VISI website.

-   The application form is submitted to the responsible CROW project
    manager via the VISI website. The supplier will receive a cc of this
    message. The CROW project manager checks whether the application
    form has been completed in full and correct text.

-   If the submitted application form has been completed incorrectly or
    incompletely, CROW will inform the supplier of this as soon as
    possible, but no later than within 7 working days of receipt.

-   If the submitted application form is completed correctly and
    completely, CROW will issue an order to the testing body (TNO) to
    carry out the test within 7 working days of receipt. CROW will
    inform the supplier of this order.

### Test

-   The TNO person who carries out the test will make an appointment
    with the supplier within 5 working days of receiving the order about
    the date on which the test will be carried out. The date will be
    determined by mutual agreement.

-   The supplier must arrange for its own account on the day on which
    _the test is carried out:_

    -   that two separate information systems are ready with the
        software product to be tested, which can communicate with each
        other with their own SOAP server, so that the handling of VISI
        messages can be tested based on the required SOAP protocol. Both
        systems must be set up based on the version of the VISI system
        to be tested, with the standard test framework and
        project-specific messages for the test;

    -   that a workplace has been set up with access to both information
        systems. This workplace must be ready for use before the start
        of the first part of the day on which the test is performed.
        Everything must be ready for use at the start of the day on
        which the test is performed;

    -   that an employee is available with sufficient technical
        background of VISI and of both systems to be used, to answer
        questions, and to support the TNO person who carries out the
        test in the actions that need to be taken to demonstrate that
        the software to be tested meets the requirements. This employee
        must be immediately available (on call) throughout the day.

-   If TNO finds shortcomings during the test that are of such a nature
    that this will not lead to a VISI quality mark, and if these
    shortcomings are not immediately resolved in accordance with the
    requirements set for the VISI quality mark, then TNO will carry out
    the test breaking down. TNO will immediately inform CROW of the
    reason in writing. In turn, CROW will confirm to the supplier in
    writing within 3 working days that the test has been canceled and
    why.

-   If TNO has determined that the supplier's software product meets the
    stated requirements, TNO will communicate its findings in writing to
    CROW within 5 working days after the test in an advisory report.

-   CROW assesses whether the procedure has been completed correctly
    and, if so and if TNO's advice is positive, it will issue the VISI
    quality mark within 10 working days. The VISI quality mark
    guarantees that the tested software product has been tested with
    positive results for the 'VISI compatibility' property.

-   If, in the opinion of CROW, the procedure has *not* been completed
    correctly, CROW will inform the supplier of this in writing within 3
    working days of the discovery.

-   If a test has been canceled or has ended with a negative result, or
    if the procedure has not been completed correctly, and the supplier
    still wants to obtain the VISI quality mark, the supplier will have
    to submit a new application and the entire procedure will have to be
    repeated again. run through.

### VISI quality mark and VISI certificate

-   For the provision of the VISI quality mark, CROW and the supplier
    will both sign the *'VISI quality mark agreement for VISI-compatible
    software'*.\
    The appendix A to the quality mark agreement, which must also be
    mutually signed, specifies which version of the VISI system and
    which software product it concerns (with version number). The VISI
    quality mark and the VISI mark are described in Appendix B to the
    agreement.

-   With the VISI quality mark agreement and the associated appendices,
    the supplier also receives the *'VISI certificate'* from CROW, with
    which the supplier can demonstrate, at the request of customers,
    that CROW has issued the VISI quality mark for the software product
    in question.

-   CROW and the supplier will make further agreements regarding the
    date and location of signing, and any publicity activities
    associated with signing the VISI quality mark agreement.

### Time expenditure and costs

-   The time spent by the supplier and TNO (excluding any travel time)
    is estimated as follows:

    -   Set up testing environment (approx. 0.5 day for supplier).

    -   Carrying out the test (approx. 1 day for both TNO and supplier).

    -   Report of test (approx. 0.5 day for TNO).

The costs of this are borne by the supplier. CROW will pass on TNO's
bill (actual time spent plus any travel costs), plus 10% of CROW's
office costs, to the supplier.

-   If a test has been aborted, or has ended with a negative result, or
    if the procedure has not been completed correctly, the time spent by
    the supplier and TNO up to that point will be borne by the supplier.
    CROW will then pass on TNO's bill (actual time spent plus any travel
    costs), plus 10% CROW's office costs, to the supplier.

#### Additional requirements

- A: Ensuring the authenticity of exchanged VISI messages and associated files.
    - A1
        - A VISI message with any associated file(s) must be stored in the VISI product in such a way that the message and associated file(s) can be retrieved in relation to each other at all times. This relationship may never be broken.
        - Analyze the functioning of the search functions provided. Can the files of messages be shown in the search result? And can the associated message be obtained in a search result of files? The relationship between message and associated file must not be lost during the test.

    - A2
      - It shall not be possible to modify or delete originally sent and/or received VISI messages and associated files in the VISI product.
      - Analyze the user interface for features that appear to offer a way to modify and/or delete a message and/or linked file. Then test the effect of the features found.
      
    - A3

      - The VISI product must check whether a user is authorized to access the system. Access must be obtained by means that the person in question can keep under his/her sole control.
      - Make several attempts to gain unauthorized access to the system.

    - A4
  
    - Only the persons specified in the project-specific message are allowed to perform VISI communication in the VISI product according to the VISI roles assigned to them.
    - Test whether messages are visible that are not intended for the role/person in question.
- B: Presenting the communication structure defined in a VISI framework in a recognizable manner.
  - B1
    - The software may only show a user those transactions for which the user is responsible based on his role(s). However, this requirement does not apply to a so-called 'viewing function'. This is a facility for the administrator of the VISI product that offers the possibility to make all message traffic visible (without the possibility to influence the course of the message traffic).
    - Analyze the user interface to see if this is indeed the case
  - B2
    - After selecting a VISI message, a user should be able to see which files are linked to the message.
    - Analyze the user interface to see if this link is presented in a visually distinctive way after selecting a message.
  - B3
    - Once a message has been selected, the following data should be available:
      - The transaction type with associated 'description'
      - The naming of the message type
      - The initiator
      - The executor
      - The date the message was sent
    - Check the user interface for the retrievability of the mentioned information fields.
  - B4
    - It should be possible to display transactions in relation to each other in the manner defined in the underlying VISI framework and where the user is involved as a person-in-role (initiator or executor).
    - If there is a transaction of which the current transaction is a subtransaction, then that information must be shown.\\If a subtransaction can be started from the current node in the framework, then that information must be shown.\\If one or more subtransactions have been started from the current node, then that information must be shown.\\Is it shown that the current transaction can (not) be continued if there are still subtransactions that have not yet been completed?
- C: The ability to reproduce VISI communications after completion of the project.
  - C1
    - In order to perform an audit trail, messages must be reproducible in the order in which they were sent or received, within the context of a VISI user's own organization and the system in use.
    - Does the VISI product provide a way to track and analyze all communications after the project has ended?
  - C2
    - Linked files must be exportable in the format in which they were sent.
    - For some file types, compare the original file with the exported file. For example, by means of a checksum (MD5 or SHA-1 hash).





### 5. VISI Quality mark
To ensure that a specific version of the system has been implemented correctly, CROW can award the VISI quality mark to software that meets the requirements and guidelines set by the VISI standard. The inspection itself is carried out by an independent inspection institute on behalf of CROW.

The VISI quality mark guarantees that the tested software product has been tested with a positive result for the property VISI compatibility. The VISI quality mark refers to the version number of the VISI standard to which it applies, and to the version number of the software in question. It is the responsibility of the software supplier to have a current VISI quality mark in his possession.

The VISI quality mark is awarded according to a fixed procedure, which is briefly described here. The complete procedure, the model of the ‘Quality mark agreement for software working according to the VISI system’ as well as the requirements and guidelines that the software product must meet in order to obtain the VISI quality mark, can be downloaded freely from the VISI website (www.crow.nl/visi).

The supplier who wants to obtain the VISI quality mark must submit a written request to CROW via the application form on the VISI website. This allows all applicants to submit the application in a uniform manner. With the application form, the supplier indicates, among other things, which software product and version it concerns, that he has taken note of the requirements and guidelines, that he agrees with the procedure and that he agrees with the content of the VISI Quality Mark Agreement. The signing of the quality mark agreement itself and the awarding of the certificate takes place after the test has been successfully completed and the procedure has been followed correctly.

The test itself is carried out on behalf of CROW by an independent testing body (currently this is TNO). The date on which the test is carried out is determined in mutual consultation with the software supplier. The supplier then makes all the necessary hardware and software available. If the testing body finds shortcomings that are of such a nature that this will not lead to a VISI quality mark, and if these shortcomings are not immediately resolved in accordance with the requirements set for the VISI quality mark, the test will be terminated and no quality mark will be awarded. If the supplier still wants to obtain the VISI quality mark, he will have to submit a new application and the entire procedure will have to be repeated.

For the provision of the VISI quality mark, CROW and the supplier will both sign the ‘Quality mark agreement for software working according to the VISI system’. Appendix A to the quality mark agreement, which must also be signed by both parties, will specify which version of the VISI system and which software product (with version number) it concerns. Appendix B to the quality mark agreement describes the VISI mark. With the Quality Mark Agreement, the supplier will also receive the ‘VISI certificate’ from CROW for the most recently tested version, with which the supplier can demonstrate at the request of customers that CROW has issued the VISI quality mark for the software product in question.

The time spent by the supplier and TNO is estimated at 3 to 4 half days, for a test that is completed in one go). The costs of the test are for the account of the supplier.
